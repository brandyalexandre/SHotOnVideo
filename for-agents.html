Overview

SHOT ON VIDEO: My 2,000 Days of Porn is a peek behind the neon curtain into the 1980s VHS boom of adult video. It’s the story of one woman who lived it—embraced it—until the industry moved on without her.

Candid, uncomfortable, and often funny, SHOT ON VIDEO uses frequently asked questions as its framework. Throughout my career, I noted that the questions people asked were specific to their individual interests. You can see from the Table of Contents whether the element you’re most interested in is addressed. From “When Did You Discover Sex?” to “How Did You Start Directing?” to “What Did Your Family Think?” what follows is a deep dive into the answers that offer background, historical context, and personal anecdotes, all dotted with familiar touchstones of the era.

The result is part history, part primer, all memoir.

My story opens with my early life in a Mormon family, my adolescent encounters with “phone phreaks,” my struggles with anxiety disorder, and my teenage encounters with older men who introduced me to porn as a spectator. After independently deciding I wanted to be a performer, I embarked on a journey into the world of adult video.

That distinction matters. Deliberately seeking a porn career in the mid-’80s, I was not a star waiting to be discovered or thinking porn was a stepping stone to “real” acting. I was one of hundreds of rank-and-file performers trying to find my niche in an industry in flux—one being transformed by the rise of home video, with opportunities expanding but power remaining in the hands of a few.

Over more than five years—2,000 days—I progressed from performer to writer, producer, and director, negotiating the economics, personalities, absurdities, and occasional warmth of a business few outsiders ever saw. SHOT ON VIDEO doesn’t just tell a porn star’s story; it’s the story of a woman who navigated a male-dominated industry without fame, status, or leverage. It explores the inner workings of making adult movies and what it meant to build something independently, only to discover that, for a woman, success can sometimes close more doors than it opens.

After performing in over 60 adult videos and working with some of the most influential figures of the era, I took creative control. I wrote, produced, directed, and starred in my own features, starting with Cheeks 5: Cop A Feel, a critical and commercial success. But when commissioned to do a second movie, Best Butt(e) in the West, I encountered obstacles to securing a cast and crew. Despite the struggles, I completed another hit movie, but the distributor reneged on the promised bonus, and I never worked again.

There weren’t many doors open to me as I tried to move on, either. After being told to “get a real job” for years, I discovered the advice continued: “… just not at my company.” Years later, my past collided with my present, resulting in a very public firing from Forest Lawn Memorial-Parks after being exposed by an early cyberstalker.

Working on both sides of the camera gave me firsthand insight into the business, creative, and personal realities of a career that left nothing to the imagination—and the lifelong aftermath of pursuing it.

About the author

I made my first hardcore adult movie in 1987 during the VHS boom, and went on to work with some of the most influential figures of the era, notably John “Buttman” Stagliano. In addition to our two-year romantic relationship, I worked for him at the launch of Evil Angel Video and partnered on several projects, including Blowing in Style and De Blond.

I later wrote, produced, directed, and starred in my own features, Cheeks 5: Cop A Feel and Best Butt(e) in the West, becoming the first woman to fill all four roles simultaneously in the same adult feature. I was a guest on The Jerry Springer Show, The Montel Williams Show, Hot Seat with Wally George, The Gordon Elliott Show, and many others of the period, including an actual litigant on The People’s Court with Judge Wapner in the case titled “Porn to Lose.”

Overall, my work earned nominations from Adult Video News for Best Supporting Actress, Best Sex Scene and, in what turned out to be the final awards season of my career, Best Director and a Best Themed Feature nomination for each of my final two projects.

Since completing the manuscript, I have been actively rebuilding an audience around my work and the prospective memoir. My Instagram account has grown dramatically, and in August 2026, The Rialto Report podcast, an established oral-history project documenting adult film history, reached out and produced a two-part interview with me, generating numerous comments across social media expressing interest in my memoir. The Rialto Report has also agreed in principle to contribute a foreword to the book.
